## What a commercial license covers

Only what the written grant names. At minimum it should state:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Scope | which versions, commits or artifacts are covered |
| Rights | install, run, modify, distribute, or host for third parties |
| Parties | the named licensee and any affiliates |
| Term | start, duration and termination conditions |
| Fee | amount or "no fee", so the arrangement is unambiguous |

- It does not weaken the PolyForm Noncommercial license for anyone else. Personal,
  study, hobby, research and noncommercial-organization use continues under that
  license without asking.
- It does not revoke the permanent MIT grant on commits made before the license change.
  See [`docs/LICENSE-HISTORY.md`](./docs/LICENSE-HISTORY.md).
- It does not grant rights over third-party work listed in
  [`LICENSES/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`](./LICENSES/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md). A commercial
  licensee still owes those upstream projects their own license terms — for example

  agreement if n8n is ever bundled rather than framed.
